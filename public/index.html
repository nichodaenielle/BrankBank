    How to play
    • Pick any card. Harder cards are worth more: $100 True/False · $200 Identification · $300 Diagrams & multi-select · $400 Calculations & short explanations · $500 Full explanations.
    • Correct = you bank the card's value. Each wrong try halves the card. After 3 wrong tries it's worth $0 and the answer is revealed.
    • True/False and 2-option cards: one try only.
    • Watch the timer. Running out of time counts as a wrong try.
    • One hidden DOUBLE DOWN card per board doubles its value!
    • 🔥 Streaks: 3 correct cards in a row = +10% bonus, 5+ in a row = +25%. A $0 card resets the streak.
    • 🛟 Lifelines (once per game): 50/50 removes wrong options, Hint gives a clue, +30s adds time.
    • 💰 Final Wager: clear the board, then bet any part of your bank on one last $500 question.
    • 🏅 Earn badges, play the Daily Challenge every day, and practise the questions you missed.
    • 🧩 Word Puzzle: solve each crossword word to bank its value. A wrong word halves it; 3 wrong tries reveal it for $0. 💡 Reveal a letter for −15%.
    • Explanations are auto-marked by checking for the key ideas.
